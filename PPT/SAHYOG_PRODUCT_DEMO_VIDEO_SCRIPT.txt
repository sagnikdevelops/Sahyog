0:00–0:25 — Opening: The Problem

SCREEN

Start on the Sahyog homepage. Show the hero section:

“Work that Works for Everyone”
“Skill. Trust. Together.”

Zoom into the headline figures:

- 1,500+ verified cooperative tradespeople
- 48+ registered labour societies
- 12,000+ services delivered
- 88% direct worker share

VOICE-OVER

“Every day, households need electricians, plumbers, caregivers, cleaners and technicians. But the current gig economy has two major gaps: customers cannot easily find trusted and verified workers, while skilled workers lose a large share of their earnings to commercial aggregators and receive very little social protection.”

“Sahyog addresses both sides of this problem by creating India’s cooperative-owned digital service marketplace.”

SCREEN

Scroll through the homepage. Show:

- Emergency Service button
- Service categories
- Cooperative-owned messaging
- Hindi/English language toggle
- Sign In and Register options

Then briefly show the presentation slide describing the problem and solution.

VOICE-OVER

“Instead of extracting 20 to 30 percent commissions, Sahyog connects customers directly with verified members of Labour Cooperative Societies.”

“The platform combines local service discovery, emergency dispatch, worker verification, transparent pricing, digital payments, welfare contributions and cooperative governance in one system.”

“The key economic model is simple: 88 percent goes directly to the worker, 7 percent supports the Cooperative Welfare and Insurance Fund, and only 5 percent maintains the digital platform.”

VOICE-OVER

“Now let us follow a complete customer workflow.”

“A customer begins with a service category, selects the exact trade, describes the problem and can attach a photo. The customer can choose a standard visit or emergency dispatch.”

“Sahyog then uses location-aware matching to find suitable cooperative workers. The matching is explainable—not a black box. Skill contributes 40 percent, proximity 30 percent, availability 20 percent and workload 10 percent.”

“The customer can compare verified workers, review ratings and experience, and select the preferred technician.”

“Before confirming, the customer sees the service price and cooperative contribution clearly. There is no advance payment. The customer pays securely after inspecting the completed work.”

“This directly tackles the customer trust gap: verified workers, predictable rates, transparent matching and a rapid emergency response target of under 30 minutes.”

RECOMMENDED VIDEO FLOW

For a strong hackathon submission:

1. Keep the opening problem statement under 50 seconds.
2. Spend the most time on the booking workflow.
3. Show the four personas using the Demo Mode switch.
4. Use zoom-ins on the 88–7–5 model and explainable matching formula.
5. Avoid showing every service card individually; show the catalog filters and a representative example.
6. End with measurable impact from the presentation:
   - 1,500+ verified workers
   - 48+ cooperative societies
   - 12,000+ projected/completed services
   - Under-30-minute emergency dispatch target
   - ₹15+ lakh projected welfare and accident fund corpus
